Unitaro Privacy Policy

Offline Unit Converter for Android

Simple. Fast. Offline.

Summary

Unitaro does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information. The app works fully offline, needs no account and requests no permissions.

Effective date: October 8, 2026  |  App version: 1.0

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy describes how the Android app Unitaro (package name com.fahadkhokhar.unitaro), published by Fahad Khokhar ("the developer", "we", "us"), handles information when you use it.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any information. Specifically, Unitaro does not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers or any account details
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone data
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs or usage analytics
  • Crash reports or diagnostics sent to us

The values you type into the converter are processed only on your device, in memory, to show the result. They are not saved or sent anywhere.

3. Permissions

Unitaro declares no permissions in its manifest.

No internet access
No location
No storage access
No contacts
No camera
No microphone

4. Clipboard

The "Copy Result" button places the converted number on your device clipboard only when you tap it. Unitaro never reads the clipboard. Newer versions of Android may show a small system message confirming that something was copied.

5. Third parties

Unitaro does not include advertising, analytics, tracking or any other third-party SDKs, and does not share data with any third party. Because the app has no network access, no data can leave your device through the app.

6. Data storage and security

Unitaro stores no personal data and keeps no user database. Android app backup is disabled, so the app does not place any data in cloud backups. Uninstalling the app removes everything the app has on your device.

7. Children's privacy

Unitaro does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Since no data is collected at all, the app is safe for all ages to use as a general utility.

8. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct, export or delete. If you have questions about your privacy rights under laws such as GDPR or CCPA, you are welcome to contact us using the details below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app changes, for example if a future version adds a feature that handles data. The latest version will always be available at this page, and the effective date above will be updated. Material changes will also be reflected in the Data safety section of the Google Play listing.
